## Proposed editorial stance

1. Reading people is weaker than people think (real-time lie judgments average 54%). The spine is a calibrated loop: notice, consider, ask, update, respond (the loop from The Reading Instrument). Asking beats imagining.
2. Cold reading is taught as a mechanism and a con (stock Barnum statements, fishing, hot reading), defence first. Its honest twin: an observation turned into a question you then verify.
3. The *American Made* lesson: Barry Seal got access by being useful and reliable to powerful people, then was murdered by cartel hitmen in 1986 after becoming a DEA informant. Legit version: be useful, be reliable, and be on one side.
4. DARK-TOPICS page pattern for Parts II and VII: mechanism, case, why smart people fall for it, red flags, defence, honest version.
