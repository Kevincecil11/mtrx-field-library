<div class="specs">
<div class="spec"><div class="spec-name">Get it, don't take it</div><p class="spec-def">25 experiments: being told to take another's perspective didn't reliably improve accuracy; getting it through conversation did. In the authors' words: understanding another mind "is therefore enabled by getting perspective, not simply taking perspective."</p></div>
<div class="spec"><div class="spec-name">Follow-ups make you liked</div><p class="spec-def">People who asked more questions, especially follow-ups, were liked more (d = .35 in the main study). A follow-up proves you listened.</p></div>
<div class="spec"><div class="spec-name">Listening lowers walls</div><p class="spec-def">Attentive, non-judgmental listening reduced defensiveness and social anxiety across five experiments. People say more true things when they feel heard.</p></div>
<div class="spec"><div class="spec-name">The curse of knowledge</div><p class="spec-def">Once you know something, it's hard to imagine not knowing it. You know websites; your dentist doesn't. Check his understanding with a question.</p></div>
