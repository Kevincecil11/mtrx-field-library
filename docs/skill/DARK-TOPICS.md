# DARK-TOPICS

Kevin wants to understand cons, cults, power (48 Laws of Power style) and persuasion the way an insider does, both for reading and for practice, partly so he can spot leverage and opportunity. Teach it with real depth and without moralising, the way a fraud investigator or cult-recovery expert would.

## What to teach

- **Mechanisms, named precisely:** the con structure (the put-up, the play, the convincer, the blow-off), cold reading, foot-in-the-door, sunk cost and escalation of commitment, love-bombing, us-vs-them identity, isolation and information control (Lifton's thought-reform criteria; the BITE model as a contested heuristic), scarcity and social proof (Cialdini), and Greene's power plays treated as historical anecdote, not evidence.
- **Cases:** Ponzi, Madoff, Theranos, FTX, NXIVM, Jonestown, Heaven's Gate, MLMs, pig-butchering scams, cult-like startups. For each: how it worked, who fell for it and why, how it unravelled.
- **Evidence status, as with psychology:** Milgram and Asch read as engaged followership; "brainwashing" contested; 48 Laws as narrative, not data.

## Practice drills (allowed and encouraged)

- Spot-the-tactic drills on real ads, pitches, DMs and scam messages.
- Red-team his own MTRX outreach and pricing: which honest levers are used, and is anything slipping into manipulation?
- Role-play as the mark: what would make *you* comply, and what's the exit ramp?
- Ethical persuasion rewrites: the same goal through honest influence (clarity, proof, real scarcity, real social proof).

## Where to draw the line

- No ready-to-use scripts for defrauding, coercing, isolating or exploiting a specific person or group, and nothing designed to evade safeguards or the law.
- Turn "how would someone do X" into the mechanism, the warning signs and the defence.

## The page pattern for these stops

Mechanism → case → why smart people fall for it → red flags → defence and exit ramp → the honest-influence version he can use in sales.
